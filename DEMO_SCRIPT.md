## SCRIPT

**[0–6 seconds] HOOK**  
97% of California's kelp forests are gone. Restoration isn’t limited by intent — it’s limited by data friction.

**[7–18 seconds] PROBLEM**  
[ACTION: Show map of California coast with fragmented data layers toggling on/off]
Today, practitioners pull from NOAA datasets, satellite imagery, KelpWatch, and multiple GIS layers that don’t talk to each other. Site selection can take weeks — and depends heavily on expert intuition.

**[19–45 seconds] DEMO**  
[ACTION: Select "California Coast" region dropdown]
Here’s BlueGrid.

[ACTION: Click "Analyze Sites"]
In seconds, we aggregate sea surface temperature, depth, nutrient levels, current velocity, and historical kelp presence into a composite Restoration Viability Score.

[ACTION: Map populates with green, yellow, and red overlays]
Green zones indicate high-probability restoration success. Yellow signals moderate viability. Red flags high environmental risk.

[ACTION: Open ranked table panel]
Each site is ranked with a clear score, key environmental drivers, and risk indicators — turning fragmented GIS data into structured, actionable guidance.

**[46–60 seconds] IMPACT & VISION**  
Three weeks of manual analysis becomes 60 seconds.  
Teams can prioritize funding, deploy faster, and scale restoration with confidence.

Today: kelp.  
Tomorrow: reefs, mangroves, and seagrass.  
BlueGrid is intelligence infrastructure for ocean restoration.
